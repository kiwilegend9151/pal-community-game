Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Pal Community Game handles information when you use our Discord bot, Twitch account linking, daily rewards and quests.

1. Information We Process

Depending on the features you use, we may process:

  • Discord information: Your Discord user ID, used to identify your account and link it to your game player.
  • Twitch information: Your Twitch account identifier and information needed to link your Twitch player.
  • Game information: Your player ID, virtual currency and item balances, daily claim records, cooldowns, quest progress and completion status.
  • Technical information: Server logs, error reports and connection information where generated by the service or its infrastructure providers.

2. How We Use Information

We use relevant information to link accounts, authenticate linking requests, award and track rewards, maintain cooldowns and quests, prevent duplicate claims, investigate abuse, protect the service, troubleshoot errors and respond to support requests.

3. Legal Basis

Where applicable data protection law requires a lawful basis, processing may be necessary to provide requested features, pursue legitimate interests such as security and fraud prevention, comply with legal obligations or rely on consent where appropriate.

4. Storage and Third Parties

Game information may be stored in the database used by Pal Community Game. The service may rely on third-party providers for hosting, database infrastructure and account authentication.

Discord and Twitch operate independently and process information under their own privacy policies. We do not control their data practices.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Information may be processed by service providers as needed to operate the game, or disclosed where required by law or reasonably necessary to protect the service and its users.

6. Security and Retention

We take reasonable steps to protect information against unauthorised access, loss or misuse. No online system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Information is retained for as long as reasonably necessary to operate player accounts, maintain game progress, enforce cooldowns, protect the service and meet applicable obligations. Retention periods may vary by information type.

7. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of or deletion of your information, object to certain processing, request restrictions or obtain a portable copy of certain data.

To make a request, contact us using the email address below. We may need to verify your identity before responding. Deletion may be subject to legal or operational retention requirements and may affect your access to game features.

If UK data protection law applies, information about your rights is available from the Information Commissioner's Office.

8. Children's Privacy

Users must meet the applicable age requirements of Discord and any connected services. If you believe information has been collected or processed inappropriately, please contact us so we can review it.

9. International Transfers

Our providers may process or store information in other countries. Where required by applicable law, appropriate safeguards should be used for international transfers.

10. Changes to This Policy
