Twitch Monsters Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 2026

Overview

Twitch Monsters is a Twitch Extension that allows viewers to participate in a community monster collecting game. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected and how it is used.

Information We Collect

  • Twitch User ID
  • Twitch Username
  • Twitch Channel ID
  • Game progress (levels, monsters, inventory, achievements)

How We Use Your Information

The information collected is used solely to:

  • Save player progress.
  • Display player collections.
  • Connect players to the correct Twitch channel.
  • Operate chat bot functionality.
  • Provide gameplay features.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties except where required by law.

Data Storage

Player information is stored securely and only for operating the Twitch Monsters game.

Your Rights

If you would like your game data removed, please contact the developer through the Twitch channel associated with the extension.

Contact

For questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer through Twitch.
